Label every finding: confirmed (a reviewer would want to change the configuration), intentional
(there is a plausible reason it is acceptable), or unclear. Subsumption: is the wildcard needed for
the subject's function? Shadowing: is there a documented reason for the gap? Cross-domain: does the
subject also need network access to pods in that namespace? Decide from the chart's documentation,
not from the tool's wording. Two people label independently. Disagreements are resolved in "final".
